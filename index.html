OUR STORY

GLOWING AMBER
SINCE DAY ONE

We undertake production and export of Ethanol (ENA) and also supply absolute alcohol (perfume grade). Our brand is synonymous with superior quality 'Liquor'. We are proud to produce a wide range of products with innovative packaging and pricing. Our understanding of the market demand and ability to effectively gauge and satisfy consumer wants has been a pivotal factor responsible for driving consistent growth.

Our comprehensive range of brands and distribution enables us to reach a wider target audience at every occasion across the world. Deejay Distilleries Pvt. Ltd made its foray into the international markets a few years ago and is today considered one of the leading exporters of bottled alcohol in regions like West Africa, Middle East and South-East Asia. In the domestic market, we have a robust and expanding distribution chain in Maharashtra and also undertake inter-state export of our brands to Pondicherry, Delhi, Daman & Diu etc.
