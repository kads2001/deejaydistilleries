OUR INFRASTRUCTURE

State-of-the-Art Distillation & Global Distribution

At Deejay Distilleries Pvt. Ltd. we are proud of our operation and production facilities which include state-of-the-art bottling lines fully equipped with tunnel bottle washing, filling, sealing and labelling machines with the capacity to produce as per the growing demand.

Our comprehensive range of brands and distribution enables us to reach a wider target audience at every occasion across the world. Deejay Distilleries Pvt. Ltd made its foray into the international markets a few years ago and is today considered one of the leading exporters of bottled alcohol in regions like West Africa, Middle East and South-East Asia. In the domestic market, we have a robust and expanding distribution chain in Maharashtra and also undertake inter-state export of our brands to Pondicherry, Delhi, Daman & Diu etc.

QUALITY & RESPONSIBILITY

Uncompromising Quality, Innovation & Social Responsibility

Consistent quality both in terms of product offering as well as packaging have been instrumental in building a strong consumer equity for our brands. Our exhaustive range of brands are the key growth drivers and comprise Whisky, Vodka, Rum, Brandy, Gin and ‘Exotic Spirits’ in varying pack sizes and alcohol strength, in order to comply with the global market requirements and enable better market penetration. Innovation and R & D form a crucial part of our growth strategy.

At Deejay Distillery Pvt. Ltd., we believe that any positive contribution to the society and environment is crucial for our sustainable growth and success. Keeping this in mind, we undertake productive Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives in the areas of education, health, helping the disabled and environment conservation.
